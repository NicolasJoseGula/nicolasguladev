Sample CSV files

The three data sets that ship inside Chart Maker, as plain CSV files. Download any of them and open it with Import CSV in the app.

figure-skating-medals.csv

Olympic figure skating medals by nation, 1924 to 2022. Twenty rows, twenty-four points in time, with two-letter country codes for the flags. The smallest file - the quickest one to try.

marketcap-history.csv

The most valuable listed companies in the world at the close of every year from 1996 to 2026, with a column of logo addresses.

premier-league-points.csv

Premier League points, cumulative, every season since 1992-93. Fifty-one clubs and a Badge column - the largest of the three.

The shape is always the same: one row per competitor, one column per point in time. An empty cell means no reading yet - not zero. A column of web addresses, or of two-letter country codes, is used as the picture on each bar.
