Privacy policy

Updated 2026-10-21

Chart Maker collects nothing, sends nothing, and has nowhere to send it.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting service and no third-party SDKs of any kind. There is no server belonging to this app for data to be sent to.

Where your work lives

Your spreadsheets, the pictures you add, your saved projects and the videos you export stay on your Mac, in the places you put them. The app reads a file when you choose it in a file panel and writes one where you tell it to. Nothing is uploaded, synced or backed up by the app.

The one thing that reaches the network

If your own CSV names a picture by web address (a club crest, a company logo, a photograph) the app fetches that picture from the address you supplied so it can draw it on the bar. That request goes to whoever hosts the image, exactly as it would if you opened the address in a browser. It happens only for addresses in files you import, and the app tells you when a picture does not arrive. Country flags and typefaces are bundled inside the app and are never fetched.

What is stored on your Mac

Two preferences: whether the welcome screen has been shown, and the list of recently opened projects. Both live in the app's own sandbox container and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app asks StoreKit whether this Apple ID owns the unlock and reads a single yes or no. It never sees your name, your email or your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

This website

The app has no analytics. This website (dezeo.lat) does: it uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages help. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it runs only after you accept it. The details, and the place to change your choice, are on the website privacy page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: dezeogsp@gmail.com.
