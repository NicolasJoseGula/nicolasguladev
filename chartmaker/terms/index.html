Plain terms for a one-payment Mac app.

The licence

You may install and use Chart Maker on the Macs associated with your Apple ID, under Apple's standard Licensed Application End User Licence Agreement. The app is licensed, not sold.

What you make with it is yours

Every video you export belongs to you, on the free version as much as the paid one. There is no claim on your data, your charts or your videos, and no attribution is required beyond the MADE WITH mark the free version draws on its exports.

The purchase

The unlock is a single non-consumable in-app purchase that removes the mark and opens the bar count. It is not a subscription and nothing renews. It is tied to your Apple ID and restores with Restore Purchases. Payment, invoicing and refunds are handled by Apple.

Your data and your images

You are responsible for the data you import and for having the right to use any picture your file points at. The app fetches the addresses you supply; it does not check who owns what is at the other end.

No warranty

The app is provided as it is. It is a tool for making videos, not a system of record: keep your own copies of your data.
