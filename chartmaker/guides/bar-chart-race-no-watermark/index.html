Making a bar chart race without a watermark

Updated 2026-08-08 · Chart Maker for Mac

What the mark is, what one payment removes, and what is identical either way.

The free version is the whole app

There is no trial, no export credit, no locked resolution and no feature that appears greyed out until you pay. The free version renders at full 4K, exports both shapes, uses every template and has no limit on how many videos you make.

What the mark actually looks like

It reads MADE WITH CHARTMAKER, set at the size of a credit line in the corner opposite the title, or along the foot when your own logo is in that corner. It is deliberately legible rather than a whisper: a mark nobody can read is not a mark, it is a stain on a free product that buys nothing.

It is drawn by the chart itself rather than composited over the video afterwards, which means it is in every frame of every export and cannot be lifted off with a filter.

Why ten bars is the free number

Ten is the house number, every published video behind the templates uses ten, so a free chart is a finished-looking chart rather than a cut-down one. Wanting eight, or fifteen, is wanting to art-direct the frame, and art direction is the thing being sold. If your file has fewer than ten competitors the free version draws as many rows as you have runners rather than leaving the bottom half of the video empty.

One payment, and that is the end of it

The unlock is a single non-consumable purchase on the App Store. No subscription, nothing recurring, no per-video cost, no cloud minutes. It is tied to your Apple ID, so it follows you to a new Mac and restores from the app menu.

Questions

Can I use free exports commercially?

Yes. The videos are yours either way - the free version simply signs them.

If I buy it, do old videos lose the mark?

No - a rendered file is finished. Export again after unlocking and the new file comes out clean.
