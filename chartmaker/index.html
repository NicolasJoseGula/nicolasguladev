How it works

Four steps from a spreadsheet to an upload. The first video takes about ten minutes; the next one takes two.

1

Pick a look

Open the app and choose one of the three templates. Each arrives with a finished chart in it.

2

Drop in your CSV

One row per competitor, one column per point in time. Your numbers replace the template’s and the styling stays.

3

Make it yours

Title, the line above it, the credit along the foot. Set the pace and watch the finished length update beside the slider.

4

Export

16:9 or 9:16, full size or half, 30 or 60fps. The panel says how many frames and roughly how long before you start.

What it looks like

The preview is the same page the exporter records, at the same shape — so what you see is what uploads.

Frequently asked questions

The things people ask before they download it.

What is a bar chart race?

An animated ranking: one bar per competitor, ordered by value, re-sorting itself as it moves through time. It is the format behind most of the “most valuable companies” and “most points in history” videos on YouTube and TikTok. Learn more →

How do I make a bar chart race video?

Pick one of the three shipped looks, import a CSV with one row per competitor and one column per point in time, edit the title and the pace, then export. Four steps, no code and no account. Learn more →

Can I export a bar chart race as an MP4?

Yes — a real video file, up to 3840×2160 at 30 or 60fps, written frame by frame rather than screen-recorded. A screen recording is at the mercy of whatever else the machine is doing and comes out the size of a window. Learn more →

How should my CSV be formatted?

One row per competitor, one column per point in time — a year, a season like 1992-93, or a date. The first column is the name. An empty cell means no reading yet, which is not the same as a zero. Learn more →

Can I make a vertical one for TikTok or Shorts?

Yes. The same project exports 16:9 for YouTube and 9:16 for Shorts, TikTok and Reels, and the vertical cut is laid out for it — wider margins so the platform’s own buttons do not sit on your bars. Learn more →

Is it free, and what is the watermark?

The app is free forever and exports at full resolution with no limit. Free exports carry a small MADE WITH mark, and one payment removes it from every video, for good. No subscription. Everything else is identical. Learn more →

Is this an alternative to Flourish?

For video, yes. Flourish is excellent for embedding an interactive chart in an article, but it has no MP4 export outside an enterprise contract, so creators screen-record it. This app hands you the file instead. Learn more →

Can I put flags, crests or logos on the bars?

Yes. 265 round country flags ship inside the app and are matched from a two-letter code, and any column of image addresses in your own file — crests, logos, faces — is drawn on the bars instead. Learn more →

Can I use data from Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. Export the sheet as CSV and import it. The reader handles what real exports contain: semicolons or tabs, decimal commas, currency symbols, Windows line endings and Excel’s byte order mark. Learn more →

Does my data leave my Mac?

No. There is no account, no cloud and no upload. The only thing the app ever fetches is a picture your own file points at by web address, and it tells you when one does not answer. Learn more →
