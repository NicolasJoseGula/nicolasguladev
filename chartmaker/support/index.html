Support

Updated 2026-08-08

Something not working, or a chart not coming out the way you expected? Start here, then write.

Email

hola@dezeo.lat — a real address, answered by the person who wrote the app. It helps if you say which macOS version you are on and, where the problem is a file, what its first two lines look like.

The three most common questions

My import came out wrong

Open View data. It shows the numbers exactly as the chart received them and lists which of your columns became the name, the picture, the flag code and the group. Almost every "the chart looks wrong" turns out to be visible there. The CSV format guide covers the layout.

Some pictures are missing from the bars

A blank badge means the address in your file did not answer — often a host that refuses requests from outside its own site. The sidebar lists the addresses that failed. See flags, crests and logos.

I paid and the mark is still there

Choose Restore Purchases from the app menu. The unlock is tied to your Apple ID rather than to the machine. Videos exported before the purchase keep their mark — export again and the new file is clean.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later. There is no iPhone, iPad or Windows version today.

Refunds

Purchases go through the App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
