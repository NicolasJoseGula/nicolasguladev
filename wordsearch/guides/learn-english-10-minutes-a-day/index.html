Can You Learn English in 10 Minutes a Day?

Guide · Updated 2026-10-30

Ten minutes a day won't make you fluent on its own, but it is enough to build a solid vocabulary steadily. What matters is doing it every day: short, regular practice beats long, rare sessions.

Why short and daily works

  • Memory needs spaced repetition: meeting a word again after a day or two.
  • A small task is easy to start, so you actually do it.
  • A daily habit adds up: a handful of new words a day becomes thousands in a few years.

A 10-minute vocabulary routine

  1. Minute 0-2: replay yesterday's words: tap the stickers and say each word.
  2. Minute 2-8: play two or three new levels of Word Search: Learning English.
  3. Minute 8-10: pick two new words and say a sentence with each one.

Keep the habit

  • Play at the same moment every day: with coffee, on the bus, before bed.
  • The daily streak in the app shows you how many days in a row you have played.
  • Medals and diplomas for each unit give you short-term goals.
