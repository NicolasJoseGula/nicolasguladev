ESL Word Search for Adults, Not Kids' Worksheets

Guide · Updated 2026-10-30

Most ESL word searches online are printable worksheets made for primary school. Adults need the same practice with real vocabulary, a sensible pace and no cartoons telling them "good job, buddy".

What adult learners need from a word search

  • Useful words. Everyday vocabulary first (food, transport, the home, work tools), then abstract and advanced words.
  • Your own language as support. A translation removes the guesswork, especially at the start.
  • Pronunciation. Adults often read English well but don't know how a word sounds.
  • Progress you can see. Words grouped by CEFR difficulty show how far your vocabulary has come (a guide, not a level test).

Using Word Search: Learning English as an adult

  1. Pick your language when you open the app; translations and menus follow it (45 languages).
  2. Play the first levels quickly to build the base A1 vocabulary.
  3. From A2 on, the game shows only the translation and the first letter. This is where real learning happens: you have to produce the English word.
  4. Use the hint (the lightbulb) only after you have tried; it reveals a letter, not the whole word.
  5. Tap stickers to hear words again and repeat them out loud.

For teachers

If you teach adults, suggest the app as homework between classes: ten minutes a day on the vocabulary of the current topic, then use the words in class conversation.

For the class itself, our free printable word searches use the same words and pictures: play one on the projector or download a PDF with its answer key, like the food word search.
