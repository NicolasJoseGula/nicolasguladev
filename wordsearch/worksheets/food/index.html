    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is this food word search free?

    Yes. You can play it here and print as many copies as you want for class or home, with no sign-up.

    How do I download it with the answer key?

    Press Download worksheet to get a PDF ready to print. Page 1 is the puzzle with its clues, page 2 is the answer key. Choose Pictures only for a harder version where students write the English word under each picture.

    What level of English is it for?

    Pick the level before you make the puzzle. A1 Beginner: APPLE, BREAD, MILK. A2 Elementary: CARROT, CHEESE, MUFFIN. B1 Intermediate: BROCCOLI, PANCAKE, CINNAMON. All levels mixes them.

    Can I get a different puzzle?

    Every New puzzle builds a new board from the 132 food words, so a class can play a different one each day.
