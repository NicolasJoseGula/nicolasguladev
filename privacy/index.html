Privacy

Website privacy

dezeo.lat · Last updated: October 21, 2026

What this page covers

This page is about the website dezeo.lat only. Each app has its own privacy policy, and most of our apps collect nothing at all: see the policies for Trip Sort, Charlatan, Guardian Reader, Block&Read, Fix Your Life, FaceCheck and Chart Maker.

Who is responsible

The website is run by Nicolás Gula, an independent developer in Montevideo, Uruguay, who decides what is measured and why. You can reach him at contact@dezeo.lat.

What we measure

The website uses Google Analytics to count visits, see which pages are read, where visitors come from (a search, a TikTok video, a link) and which App Store or Google Play buttons get tapped. We use it only to learn which pages help people find our apps.

What Google receives

  • Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_*) to tell a returning visitor from a new one.
  • It receives the page address, the page you came from, your browser, device type and language, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses.
  • Google signals and every advertising feature are turned off. The data is not used for ads and is not shared with advertisers.
  • Data is kept for 14 months, then deleted.

Google processes this data under Google's privacy terms.

Legal basis

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google Analytics runs only with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time below. Elsewhere we rely on our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used, always with the option to turn it off.

Where the data goes

The data is processed by Google LLC, mostly in the United States. Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and also applies the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses to these transfers.

Your choice

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google Analytics stays off until you accept it in the banner. Everywhere else it runs by default, and you can turn it off here. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics never runs.

Your current choice: checking…

Turning it off stops new measurement right away. To remove cookies already set, clear this site's data in your browser.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access or delete the data about your visits, object to its use, withdraw your consent at any time and complain to your data protection authority. The data is tied only to a random cookie ID, not to your name or email, so the fastest way to delete it is to clear this site's data in your browser. If you prefer, write to us and we will help; we answer within 30 days.

Other content on the site

  • The Trip Sort pages load their typeface from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to serve it.
  • The videos on the Trip Sort page use TikTok's own player, which loads only when you tap a video and is covered by TikTok's privacy policy.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server access logs, including IP address and user agent, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not read, receive or have access to those logs.
