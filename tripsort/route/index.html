Trip Sort World Route

The order a player travels in, stop by stop, on a real map. Each stop is a place with 12 levels; finishing them completes its postcard and stamps the passport. The trip starts in Montevideo, goes round the whole world and ends in Stanley (Falkland Islands), back on the same side of the Atlantic where it began.

  • 255stops
  • 192UN countries
  • 20chapters
  • 3,060levels
StartEnd· Zoom and drag like any map; switch to satellite at the top right. Tap a chapter to fly to it, or a dot for its stop.

Levels count 12 per stop: stop 1 is levels 1–12, stop 2 is 13–24, and so on.
