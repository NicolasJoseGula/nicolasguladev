Trip Sort

Privacy Policy

Trip Sort for iOS and Android · Last reviewed: October 3, 2026

Summary

Trip Sort has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress, coins, boosters and settings are saved only on your device, and we never receive them.

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google receives some technical information from your device, as explained in section 4. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Trip Sort game for iOS and Android published by Nicolas Gula ("we", "us"), and to this page at dezeo.lat/tripsort.

It does not cover the App Store or Google Play themselves. Your download, purchases and payments are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, or by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Data we collect

We collect no personal data. Trip Sort has no sign-in, no account, no backend and no database. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information, and the game sends nothing to us.

The game contains no analytics service of ours. Anything it records about your play is kept on your device, as described in the next section.

3. What the game stores on your device

Everything in this section stays on your phone or tablet and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your progress: your current level, the destinations, postcards and landmarks you have unlocked, and your stars and best scores.
  • Your game items: your coins and boosters, and which one-time purchases (such as Remove Ads or the Starter Pack) you have already made, so the game can apply them.
  • Daily features: your daily gift calendar, your daily mission progress and streak, your daily challenge results and trophies, and your achievements.
  • Your settings: sound, vibration and the language you chose.

None of this identifies you. Deleting the game removes all of it, immediately and permanently. Because we keep no copy, we cannot restore lost progress for you. Purchases can always be restored from the store with the "Restore Purchases" button in the shop.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Trip Sort is free to play and is supported by ads delivered by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The game shows two kinds of ads: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a reward, and full-screen ads between some levels.

To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and measure their performance, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects technical information and sends it directly to Google. That information can include:

  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location can be inferred.
  • Device information, such as model, operating system version and language.
  • Information about the ads shown and how you interact with them.
  • Diagnostic information, such as crashes and performance of the ad code.
  • Your device's advertising identifier, where the operating system makes it available. See below.

On iOS, Trip Sort does not ask for permission to track you, so Google does not receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) from the app.

On Android, Google may use your device's advertising ID to show more relevant ads. You can reset it or delete it at any time in your device settings, under Google → Ads.

This information is processed by Google under its own policies, not by us, and we never receive it in a form that identifies you. To learn more, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can stop the ads that appear between levels by buying Remove Ads in the shop. Rewarded ads remain available because you choose when to watch them.

5. Purchases

Coin packs, the Starter Pack and Remove Ads are in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or receive your name, payment method or account details. The game only learns whether a purchase succeeded, so it can add your coins or boosters, or turn off ads. "Restore Purchases" asks the store to send your past purchases again.

6. Permissions

Trip Sort does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. It uses an internet connection only to load ads and to talk to the App Store or Google Play when you make or restore a purchase. You can play offline; ads and purchases simply won't be available then.

7. Your rights and controls

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete, port and restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to its sale. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete, and nothing to sell.

For data processed by Google in connection with ads, you can use the controls in your device settings described in section 4, and the tools Google provides at myaccount.google.com.

If you have any question, contact us at gulabonzinillc@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. The game data described in section 3 stays on your device until you delete the game. Ad-related data is kept by Google according to its own retention policies.

9. Children

Trip Sort is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game itself collects none from any user. If you believe a child's information has reached us, contact us at gulabonzinillc@gmail.com.

10. This website

This page is served as static files. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds no third-party trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server access logs, including IP address and user agent, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not read, receive or have access to those logs.

11. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated before the change ships, together with the date above. Material changes will also be mentioned in the release notes on the App Store and Google Play.
