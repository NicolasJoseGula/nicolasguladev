Privacy Policy

Trip Sort for iOS and Android · Last updated: October 27, 2026

Summary

Trip Sort has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress, coins, boosters and settings live only on your device. We never receive them, and we cannot see who you are.

To fix crashes and make the game better, it sends us play statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase, tied to a random identifier rather than to you (section 3).

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google's advertising software inside the game receives some technical information from your device, described in section 5. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the game sends your achievements and your Weekly Tournament points to that service, so they show up there and on its leaderboard. This is optional, and the whole game works without it (section 7).

You can play offline. Without a connection there are simply no ads and no store.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Trip Sort game for iOS and Android ("the game", "the Services") and to this page at dezeo.lat/tripsort. The game is published by Nicolas Gula, an independent developer based in Uruguay ("we", "us"). For the purposes of data protection law, we are the controller of any personal data processed through the game.

It does not cover the App Store or Google Play themselves. Your download, purchases and payments are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, or by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

2. The short version

  • We never learn who you are. The game has no account and no database of ours; the only sign-in is the optional one to Apple's Game Center or Google Play Games (section 7). What reaches us is play statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase, linked only to a random identifier (section 3).
  • Everything about your game stays on your device (section 4). Deleting the game deletes it.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob. Google's software collects technical data to serve and measure ads (section 5). We never receive that data in a form that identifies you.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, and checked through RevenueCat (section 6). We only learn whether a purchase succeeded.
  • No chat, no messages, no user content. The only thing other players can see is your Weekly Tournament score on the Game Center or Google Play Games leaderboard, and only if you play signed in to that service (section 7).

3. Data we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, biometric data, or anything else that tells us who you are. What does reach us comes through Google Firebase, a set of tools from Google LLC that processes the data on our behalf, and is limited to the following.

Play statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Only after you accept the Terms of Use and this policy when you first open the game, it records how the game is played: levels started, won or lost and how long they took, boosters used and coins spent, rewarded ads watched, daily gifts and missions claimed, the tutorial, achievements, and that a purchase was delivered (never your payment details). With these events Google collects a random app-instance identifier created when the game is installed; the device model, operating system, language and app version; the number and length of sessions; an approximate location (country, region or city) derived from your IP address, which Google Analytics does not store; and, from the stores, the product and price of purchases.

We see this mostly as totals, for example how many players finish level 10 or which level makes people give up, and we use it to balance the levels, decide what to improve and check that a change made the game better. On Android, the game stops Analytics from reading the advertising ID. On iOS, Analytics can read the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allowed tracking in Apple's prompt. If you turn off Personalized ads in Settings, the statistics are not used to personalize ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, play statistics are stored only if you agree to it in the consent message the game shows before any ads (the purpose "Store and/or access information on a device"), and they are used for advertising only if you agree to that too. If you decline, Google Analytics receives only signals without identifiers, from which it can estimate totals. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or runs into an error, it sends a report with the technical trace of the error, the device model, operating system, free memory and storage, screen orientation, app version, and a random installation identifier. Crash reports contain no game progress and nothing you wrote. We use them only to find and fix bugs.

Values we can adjust (Firebase Remote Config)

When it starts, the game asks Firebase for a few numbers we can adjust without an update, such as how often ads appear between levels or what boosters cost in coins. Sometimes we compare two versions of such a number, and the game is then placed in one of two groups at random. For this Firebase receives a random installation identifier, the app version, language and country.

If you write to us for support, we receive what you choose to send: your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed to resolve your request, then delete it. Please do not send us personal information we do not need.

The game is not designed to collect sensitive personal data (such as health, religion, political opinions or sexual orientation). If any were ever sent to us inadvertently, we would delete it promptly.

4. What the game stores on your device

Everything in this section stays on your phone or tablet, in the game's own private storage. It is never transmitted to us, and there is no server it could be transmitted to. (Your Weekly Tournament points are counted here too; if you are signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games, they are also sent to that service, as described in section 7.) (The statistics in section 3 are separate: they describe what happens in the game, not your saved game.)

  • Your progress: your current level, the destinations, postcards and landmarks you have unlocked, your stars and best scores.
  • Your game items: your coins and boosters, and which one-time purchases (such as Remove Ads or the Starter Pack) you have already made, so the game can apply them.
  • Daily features: your daily gift calendar, daily mission progress and streak, daily challenge results and trophies, and your achievements.
  • Your settings: sound, vibration and the language you chose, and whether you have seen the tutorial.
  • Small bookkeeping values: for example, the date the game last asked you for a rating, so it does not ask again too soon.

None of this identifies you. Deleting the game removes all of it, immediately and permanently. Because we keep no copy, we cannot restore lost progress for you, and progress does not transfer between devices. Purchases can always be restored from the store with the "Restore Purchases" button in the shop.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Trip Sort is free to play and is supported by ads delivered by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The game shows two kinds of ads: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a reward, and full-screen ads between some levels. You can stop the ads between levels by buying Remove Ads in the shop; rewarded ads remain available because you choose when to watch them.

What Google's software collects

To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and detect fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the game collects technical information and sends it directly to Google. It can include:

  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location (country, region or city) can be inferred. We never receive your IP address.
  • Device information: model, operating system version, language, screen size, time zone, network type.
  • Information about the ads shown and how you interact with them (views, clicks, whether a rewarded ad was completed).
  • Diagnostic information about the ad software, such as crashes and performance.
  • Your device's advertising identifier, where the operating system makes it available (see below).

Google may use this information to show you personalized ads, based on your interests inferred across apps, or non-personalized ads, based only on the context (for example, the game you are playing and your approximate location). Google processes it under its own policies, not ours. To learn more, see how Google uses data when you use its partners' sites or apps and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • On iOS, the first time you open the game it asks for permission to track you, using Apple's standard prompt. If you decline, Google does not receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) from the game and shows you non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings under Google → Ads. The game then shows non-personalized ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalized ads are only shown with your consent. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your choice before loading any ads, and you can change it later from the game's settings (Settings → Privacy options). Everywhere else, the Personalized ads switch in Settings lets you turn personalization off at any time. Declining does not limit the game; you simply see non-personalized ads.
  • Everywhere, buying Remove Ads stops the ads between levels.

Choosing non-personalized ads does not remove ads: the game still needs them to stay free, but Google then serves them without using your advertising identifier for personalization.

6. Purchases

Coin packs, the Starter Pack, the Piggy Bank, the Trip Pass and Remove Ads are in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or receive your name, payment method, card number or account details, and we do not store any payment information. The game only learns whether a purchase succeeded, together with the product identifier, so it can add your coins or boosters, or turn off ads. "Restore Purchases" asks the store to send your past purchases again.

To check each purchase with Apple or Google and tell the game what to deliver, the game uses RevenueCat, a service of RevenueCat, Inc. that acts as our processor. RevenueCat receives a random identifier it creates for this copy of the game (not your name or account), the store's record of each purchase (product, price and currency, date, transaction number and storefront country), and technical details such as the app version, the operating system and the device's IP address, from which an approximate country can be inferred. It never receives your payment details. We can see this purchase history in RevenueCat, tied only to that random identifier.

Coins and boosters are virtual items with no monetary value outside the game. They cannot be exchanged for money, transferred to other players or refunded by us; refund requests for a purchase go to Apple or Google, which handle them under their own rules.

7. Game Center and Google Play Games

The game works fully without these services. If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone or iPad, or to Google Play Games on Android, the game uses that service for:

  • The Weekly Tournament: your points for the current week (one for each star you win, three for a Daily Challenge or an event door) are sent to the tournament's leaderboard, where you and other players see the ranking with your Game Center or Play Games nickname and picture. The game also reads your place on the leaderboard to show it to you. The tournament's chests depend only on the points counted on your device, so you can win them without signing in.
  • Achievements (Game Center on iOS): when you earn one, the game tells Game Center so it appears in your Game Center profile.

Signing in, your nickname, picture and friends are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. We never receive your name, email address or account details. Like any player, we can see the leaderboard, with each player's nickname and score. On Android, the game asks Google only for the permission Google Play Games needs for its leaderboards; it does not use Google Drive or any other data in your Google account, and the game's use of that data follows Google's API Services User Data Policy. To stop sharing, sign out of Game Center (iPhone or iPad Settings → Game Center) or of Google Play Games, or change who can see your activity in their settings.

8. Ratings prompt

After a happy moment in the game, it may invite you to rate it using the standard rating window of the App Store or Google Play. That window is run by Apple or Google, not by us: we never learn whether you rated the game, or what you wrote. The game only remembers the date it last asked, so it does not ask again for months.

10. Children

Trip Sort is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, or under the age at which your country lets you consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game collects nothing that identifies any user. Ads in the game are not targeted at children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the game: everything the game stores is on the device, so deleting the game from the device erases it completely. If you believe any information about a child has reached us or Google through the game, contact us at contact@dezeo.lat and we will help you, including by pointing you to Google's own tools for the advertising data described in section 5.

12. Where data is processed

We keep no servers of our own. The statistics and crash reports in section 3 are stored by Google on its Firebase infrastructure; the advertising, store, Game Center and Google Play Games data described above is processed by Google and Apple on their own infrastructure; and purchase records are processed by RevenueCat. These include servers in the United States and other countries whose laws may differ from yours. For data from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google, Apple and RevenueCat rely on recognized transfer safeguards, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in their own privacy policies linked in section 9.

13. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to receive a copy of it, to restrict or object to its processing, to withdraw a consent you gave, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

In practice: we hold nothing that tells us who you are. The Firebase data in section 3 is tied to a random identifier that we cannot connect to you, so we cannot pick out which records are yours; it is deleted automatically after the periods in section 14, and deleting the game stops all collection (a new install starts with a new identifier). If you object to the statistics, write to us and we will help. Everything in section 4 is on your own device and under your sole control, and deleting the game removes it. For the advertising data processed by Google, the controls in section 5 let you reset or remove your advertising identifier and switch to non-personalized ads, and Google's own tools at myaccount.google.com let you review and delete activity linked to a Google account.

You can still write to us at contact@dezeo.lat with "Privacy request" in the subject. We answer within 30 days (45 days for requests under California law), and we may ask you to confirm details so that we do not act on someone else's request. You may also use an authorized agent where your law allows it, and you can appeal our answer by replying to it. We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

In addition to the rights above, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority: the authority of your EU country, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or the Swiss FDPIC.

California

We do not sell personal information. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, the disclosure of an advertising identifier to Google for personalized advertising may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out of it at any time with the device controls in section 5 (declining tracking on iOS, or deleting your advertising ID on Android), after which the game shows only non-personalized ads. We do not knowingly share the personal information of anyone under 16. California residents also have the right to know what personal information is collected, used and shared, as described in sections 3 to 9. You may also use an authorized agent to make a request for you; the agent has to show your signed permission, and we may ask you to confirm the request yourself. On the game's website, a Global Privacy Control signal turns Google Analytics off (see section 16).

Other U.S. states

Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and the other states with comprehensive privacy laws can ask to access, correct, delete or get a copy of their personal data, and can opt out of targeted advertising. Most of what is described in section 3 never reaches us, so most of these requests are handled on your device: deleting the game removes everything it stored, and the device controls in section 5 stop personalized ads. For anything else, write to us and we will answer within 45 days.

If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the word "Appeal" in the subject. We will respond within 60 days, explaining what we did and why. If you are still not satisfied, you can contact the attorney general of your state.

Brazil

Under the LGPD, you have the rights listed above, including confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization or deletion, portability, and information about sharing. You may also complain to the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

Uruguay

Under Law 18.331, you have the rights of access, rectification, update, inclusion and deletion, and you may complain to the Unidad Reguladora y de Control de Datos Personales (URCDP).

14. Data retention

The game data described in section 4 stays on your device until you delete the game. Play statistics are kept at event level for 2 months and then only as totals; crash reports are kept for 90 days. Support emails are deleted once your request is resolved, unless a law requires us to keep them longer. Advertising, store and leaderboard data is kept by Google and Apple according to their own retention policies; each week's tournament leaderboard is replaced by a new one, and Apple and Google decide how long past weeks remain visible. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as we use the service, so that purchases can be restored.

15. Security

The game communicates with Google, Apple and RevenueCat only over encrypted connections, and it stores nothing of yours anywhere but your device. We keep no database of our own; the Firebase data is stored by Google and can be reached only through our Google account. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, however; if we ever learned of a security incident affecting the game's users, we would publish a notice on this page and in the game's release notes, and notify the authorities where the law requires it.

16. This website

This website (dezeo.lat) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read and which store buttons are tapped. It sets first-party cookies. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it runs only after you accept it, and it never runs if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. Google signals and advertising features are off. The game videos on the Trip Sort page use TikTok's player, which loads only when you tap a video and is covered by TikTok's privacy policy, and the pages load their typeface from Google Fonts. The details, and the place to change your choice, are on the website privacy page. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server access logs, including IP address and user agent, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not read, receive or have access to those logs.

None of this happens inside the game, whose data use is described in the sections above.

17. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, for example because a new service is added, this page will be updated before the change ships, and the "last updated" date at the top will change with it. Material changes will also be mentioned in the game's release notes on the App Store and Google Play. Continuing to use the game after a change means you have read the updated policy; if you disagree with it, you can stop using the game and delete it, which also deletes everything it stored.
