FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I add subtitles to a video on my iPhone?

Open Charlatan, pick a clip from your library or record one in the app, and it transcribes the audio on the device. Choose a font and a pace, drag the subtitle where you want it, and export. The captions are burned into the video file, so they show up on every platform.

Learn more

What is the best font for subtitles?

For video watched on a phone, a heavy condensed sans with a strong outline reads best: Impact, Bebas Neue, or a bold grotesque. Weight and contrast matter more than the typeface itself, because the caption has to survive a bright background and a small screen.

Learn more

Can I make word-by-word karaoke captions?

Yes. Charlatan has three paces (one word at a time, short punches of two or three words, or full sentences) and a karaoke highlight that lights each word up at the exact moment it is spoken. That is the style most short-form creators use.

Learn more

Are the subtitles burned into the video or a separate file?

Burned in. Charlatan renders the captions into the exported frames, so they survive being uploaded anywhere and look identical on every device. There is no SRT file to attach or lose.

Learn more

Does Charlatan work without internet?

Yes. The speech recognition runs on the iPhone itself, so transcription, styling and export all work with no connection. There is no account, no upload and no server: the audio never leaves your phone.

Learn more

Can I translate my subtitles into another language?

Yes. Record in one language and export the subtitles in another: twenty languages are available. You choose the audio language before transcribing, then translate the finished captions before export.

Learn more

Can I add captions to Instagram Reels and TikTok?

Yes. Export at 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, 1:1 for the feed, or 16:9 for wide. Because the captions are burned in, they stay exactly as you styled them instead of being replaced by each platform's own caption tool.

Learn more

Is there a watermark on exported videos?

The free version adds a small Charlatan mark in the top-left corner. Charlatan Pro removes it. Nothing else about the export changes: same resolution, same quality.

Learn more

How many fonts does Charlatan have?

99, grouped the way people search for them (Bold, Aesthetic, Condensed, Cute, Cursive, Serif, Typewriter, Pixel, Comic, Gothic) and you preview each one on your own video before choosing. 17 are free; the rest come with Charlatan Pro.

Learn more

Where should I put the subtitles in the frame?

Lower-middle third, above the platform's own interface. On Shorts and Reels the bottom fifth of the screen is covered by buttons and the caption text, so anything down there gets hidden. Charlatan lets you drag the subtitle anywhere and shows you exactly where it lands.
