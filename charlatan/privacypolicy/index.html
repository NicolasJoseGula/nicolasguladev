Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-10 · Charlatan for iPhone

The short version

Charlatan has no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. Your videos, your audio and your subtitles stay on your iPhone.

Speech recognition

Charlatan transcribes your video's audio using Apple's speech recognition, and requires it to run entirely on your iPhone. The app only offers the languages the device can transcribe on its own; there is no server option and no fallback to one, so the audio never leaves your phone.

Camera, microphone and photo library

The camera and microphone are used only when you record a video inside the app. Photo library access is used only to pick the videos you choose and to save your exports. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Charlatan does not collect, detect, analyse, derive or store face data of any kind. The app performs no face detection, face recognition, face tracking or facial-geometry mapping, and it does not use ARKit, the Vision framework, Face ID or any other biometric API. A video you record or import is treated as a video file: its audio is transcribed on the device, and the frames are only decoded to show a preview and to render your subtitles into the exported file. No face data is created, retained, shared with third parties, or transmitted off your iPhone, because none is ever collected.

Translation

Subtitle translation uses Apple's Translation framework, which may download language models and processes your subtitle text according to Apple's privacy policy. Charlatan does not send your text to any server of its own — it has none.

Data stored on your device

Your projects — the video references, transcripts and styles — are stored locally on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete them or uninstall the app. We have no copy and no way to access them.

Purchases

Charlatan Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app checks your entitlement with Apple's StoreKit, on the device.

Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs, no trackers and no ad networks in the app. The only external party involved is Apple, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before that version ships, with the new effective date above.
