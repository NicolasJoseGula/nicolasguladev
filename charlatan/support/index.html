Support

Charlatan for iPhone · iOS 18 or later

Common questions

Most questions (fonts, karaoke captions, translation, watermark, exporting for each platform) are answered in the FAQ and the guides.

The transcription missed words or the timing is off

Check that the audio language selected on the home screen matches the language spoken in the video: that is the most common cause. Clear speech close to the microphone transcribes best; heavy background music or several people talking at once lowers accuracy. Every word can be corrected in the Text panel before exporting.

I bought Pro and it looks locked

Open the Pro screen and tap "Restore purchases". If it still shows locked, make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account that made the purchase, then restart the app.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or the subscriptions page. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Requirements

Charlatan requires an iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Transcription runs on the device for the major languages; no internet connection or account is needed.
