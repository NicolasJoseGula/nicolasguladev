Hormozi-style captions on iPhone

Updated 2026-10-07 · Charlatan for iPhone

People search this after a clip where every line hit like a headline. It is not a filter: it is five decisions, and once you can name them you can make any video read that way.

What the look is made of

  • Tall, heavy capitals, Anton or something like it, with a thick black outline.
  • One to three words on screen at a time.
  • One word per line in colour, usually yellow or green: the word that carries the point.
  • Each word arriving as it is said: rising or popping in, not the whole line at once.
  • Centred, a little below the middle, clear of the app's buttons.

Why the coloured word matters most

Someone scrolling with the sound off reads one word per line, not three. Colouring the right one (the number, the noun, the verb that does the work) means the skim still gets the message. Colour the wrong one, or a different one every line, and it turns into noise.

How to do it in Charlatan

  1. Open your clip and choose the Punch pace: bursts of two to three words.
  2. Open the Styles tab and tap Hustle. Font, outline, the yellow keyword and the rising words are set at once.
  3. The keyword is picked for you in each line. To change it, go to Text, tap the pencil on a subtitle and tap the word you want.
  4. Drag the caption into place (the guide shows where TikTok, Reels and Shorts buttons will cover it) and export at 9:16.

Questions people also ask

What font are Hormozi captions?

A tall, condensed, heavy sans in capitals. Anton is the free font closest to it; Impact and Bebas Neue are near relatives. The font matters less than the capitals, the outline and the single coloured keyword.

Is the Hustle style made by or affiliated with Alex Hormozi?

No. Hustle is Charlatan's own style, built from the elements that look is known for. The name is only used here to describe the look people search for.
