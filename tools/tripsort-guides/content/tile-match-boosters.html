Tile match boosters are one-tap helpers that get you out of a tight spot: a hint shows a good move, undo takes back a mistake, shuffle mixes the board, a magic wand clears a set and an extra slot gives the tray more room. Used at the right moment, one booster can save a level you were about to lose; used too early, it is gone when you really need it.

Why boosters matter in a tile match game

In a triple tile match, you tap tiles into a small tray and clear them in sets of three. In Trip Sort the tray has 4 slots, and if it fills up with no match, the level is lost. Most losses do not come from one terrible move. They come from a slow build-up: a couple of single tiles sitting in the tray, a pair you cannot finish because the third tile is buried, and then one tap too many.

Boosters are built for exactly those moments. Each one solves a different kind of problem, so the trick is not just having them, but knowing which one fits the trouble you are in. The five tile match boosters, one by one

Hint: when you cannot see the next move

The tile match hint lights up your best move. It is the gentlest booster, because it does not change the board; it only points. Use it when the board looks crowded and you keep scanning without spotting a third matching tile, or when you are about to make a risky tap and want a second opinion.

Undo: when you tapped the wrong tile

Undo puts your last tile back on the board. It is the fix for a slip of the finger or a move you regret right away, for example picking a tile that does not match anything in the tray. Undo is most valuable when you notice the mistake immediately, before you build more moves on top of it.

Shuffle: when the board is stuck

Shuffle mixes up the tiles left on the board. It does not touch the tray, so it helps most when the tray is fairly empty but the tiles you can reach do not line up with each other. A shuffle can bring buried matches to the surface and turn a dead-end board into a playable one.

Magic Wand: when you need a set gone

The Magic Wand clears a whole set for you. It is the strongest single fix, because it removes three tiles without using any tray space. Save it for the moments when the tray is nearly full and nothing on top of the board can complete what is already in it.

Extra Slot: when you need breathing room

The Extra Slot adds a fifth slot to the tray. Going from 4 to 5 slots is a big change: it gives you room to pick up a tile you need to uncover something else, without the tray filling up. It works well on layered boards where the tiles you need sit under several others.

When to use each booster: a quick guide

A simple way to decide is to look at the tray first, then the board:

  • You just made a mistake: Undo. It is the cheapest way to get back to where you were.
  • Tray mostly empty, board looks hopeless: Shuffle, or a Hint if you suspect there is a move you are missing.
  • Tray almost full, one slot left: Magic Wand or Extra Slot. The wand clears a set right away; the extra slot buys you time to dig.
  • Deep layers, the tile you need is buried: Extra Slot early, before the tray gets tight.

A few habits make your boosters last longer:

  1. Do not use one on easy boards. Early boards are calm and easy. Learn the rules there and keep your boosters for the layered levels later on.
  2. Take your time. There are no timers, so pause and scan the whole board before you tap. Many "I need a hint" moments disappear after a calm second look.
  3. Use one booster, then reassess. Stacking several in a row often wastes them. After each one, look at the new board before deciding on the next.
  4. Remember the retry. Losing a level is not the end: you can retry it, and sometimes a fresh start is better than spending a booster on a board that already went wrong.

How to get more boosters without paying

You start Trip Sort with some of each booster, and you can buy more with coins. Coins are part of the regular game, along with daily rewards, daily missions, a Daily Challenge and achievements, so playing a little every day keeps you moving. A few more options:

  • Rewarded videos for extra hints. They are always optional; watch one only if you want the extra help.
  • Continue after a loss. If the tray fills up, you can continue with a rewarded video or coins instead of starting over.
  • Replay finished cities. Open the Journey, tap a destination you completed and pick any of its 12 levels, which is a relaxed way to keep playing without pressure.

Ads and rewarded videos need a connection. Every level still plays offline, but offline there are no ads and the Shop is not available, so plan your coin spending before a flight. Our triple tile match basics guide is a good refresher on the tray rules before you dive into harder boards.

How to do it in Trip Sort

  1. Open a level and check the tray: count how many of the 4 slots are taken and what you still need to complete.
  2. If you cannot find a move, tap Hint to light up your best move.
  3. If you tapped the wrong tile, tap Undo to put it back on the board.
  4. If nothing on the board matches, tap Shuffle to mix up the tiles that are left.
  5. If the tray is nearly full, use the Magic Wand to clear a set, or the Extra Slot to get a fifth slot.
  6. When you run low, buy more boosters with coins, and keep up with daily rewards, daily missions and the Daily Challenge.
