Inside the App

Nine cards. One honest reading.

Your score

A score out of 1000. For the photo, not the person.

Symmetry, proportions, capture quality and aesthetics combine into one number, plus where your photo lands next to others. Every point you're missing is something a better shot can win back.

Every feature, measured

First impression

What your face conveys in the first two seconds.

Calm, bright, intense, thoughtful: your expression is measured from real geometry (smile curve, eye openness, brow line) and always described in the positive.

Your facial signature

Golden-ratio proportions, described and never graded.

Eye spacing, mouth width, face shape: the measurements that make your face yours and nobody else's. These don't score anything. They tell you your shape, in words.

Your shot's angle

Straight-on, three-quarter, chin up: every angle says something different, and FaceCheck tells you what yours is saying and why it works.

Points you can gain

Concrete tips. Real points. Next photo, better score.

More front light, a straighter angle, contrast with the background, a slight smile. FaceCheck counts the exact points on the table. Everything improves from the photo; your face is already fine as it is.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a face symmetry test?
It measures how closely the two halves of your face mirror each other: eye level, brow line, mouth corners, jaw contour. FaceCheck runs it from one photo using 76 facial landmarks, entirely on your iPhone. No face is perfectly symmetrical, and it shouldn't be.
How does FaceCheck score my photo?
Four measurements (symmetry, proportions, capture quality and aesthetics) combine into a score out of 1000, plus where your photo lands next to others. The score describes the photo, not the person.
Is my photo uploaded anywhere?
No. The analysis runs entirely on your iPhone with Apple's on-device Vision framework. No upload, no servers, no account. It even works in airplane mode.
What is the golden ratio face?
A classical ideal of facial proportion (about 1.618) covering eye spacing, mouth width and face shape. FaceCheck measures your real ratios and describes your facial signature: it describes, it doesn't grade.
What is a selfie rating?
It tells you how a photo reads before you post it: what it conveys in two seconds, which feature carries it, and what would make the next shot stronger. FaceCheck rates the photo, never the person.
Can I improve my score?
Yes, that's the point. FaceCheck shows the exact points you can gain and the tips to get them: front light, straighter angle, simpler background, slight smile. Everything improves from the photo.
Does FaceCheck work offline?
Yes. The whole analysis runs on your device, so FaceCheck works in airplane mode. No connection, no account, no sign-up.
How much does FaceCheck cost?
Free: your percentile, what your photo conveys, your best feature and your personalized reading headline. Full analysis: a one-time purchase from $2.99: score breakdown, every feature measured, facial signature and photo tips. No subscription, nothing to cancel.
Is FaceCheck accurate?
It measures real geometry (76 landmarks, symmetry deviations, proportion ratios) with the same Apple Vision technology behind Portrait mode. And it's honest about its limits: FaceCheck is for entertainment, not a medical or diagnostic tool.
What actually makes a photo attractive?
Mostly things you control: even front light, a deliberate angle, an uncluttered background, a relaxed expression. Those show up in FaceCheck as capture quality, angle and framing, usually the fastest points to gain.
