Privacy Policy

FaceCheck for iOS · Effective date: [DATE OF PUBLICATION] · Last reviewed: August 26, 2026

Summary

FaceCheck collects no personal data. The app contains no network code, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party libraries of any kind. Every photo you analyze is processed on your device and is never transmitted, and the app has no server to transmit it to. The only information the app stores is three non-identifying preferences held on your own device.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the FaceCheck iOS application published by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we", "us"), and to this website at dezeo.lat/facecheck.

It does not cover the App Store itself. Your download, purchase and payment are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and we never receive your payment details.

2. Photos: what happens to them

When you take a photo with the camera or select one through the system photo picker, FaceCheck analyzes it using Apple's on-device Vision framework: it detects the face, measures 76 facial landmarks, and derives symmetry, proportion, angle and expression values.

  • Your photo is never uploaded. The application contains no networking code whatsoever, so it is not technically capable of transmitting your image.
  • Your photo is never written to storage by the app. The image and its analysis exist only in the app's working memory while you are viewing the result, and are discarded when you analyze another photo or close the app.
  • The app has no server, no database and no cloud storage. There is no backend, so there is no copy of anything anywhere.
  • It works in airplane mode. This is the practical proof of everything above.

Because the app never receives or retains your photo, we cannot access, disclose, sell, share or be compelled to produce it.

3. Data we collect

None. FaceCheck collects no personal information, no biometric identifiers, no usage data, no device identifiers, no crash reports and no advertising identifiers. It contains no analytics or telemetry of any kind.

This corresponds to the "Data Not Collected" declaration on the app's App Store privacy label.

4. What the app stores on your device

FaceCheck saves three preferences locally, in the standard iOS preferences store, so the app behaves the way you left it. None identify you, and none ever leave your device:

  • Your chosen interface language, if you set one in Settings.
  • Whether you have seen the introduction, so it is not shown again.
  • The purpose you selected for your photo (for example "work profile" or "social media"), which changes which tips the app shows you.

Deleting the app removes all three, immediately and permanently.

5. Permissions the app requests

  • Camera. Requested only when you choose to take a photo, and used only to capture the image you are about to analyze on-device.
  • Photo library. FaceCheck uses the system photo picker, which shares only the individual photos you explicitly select. The app is never granted access to your library as a whole and does not request that permission.

6. On-device intelligence

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, FaceCheck may use Apple's on-device language model to write your personalized reading. Only the numeric measurements already computed on your device (for example your score, angle and strongest feature) are passed to that model; your photo is not. This processing also happens on your device, under Apple's own privacy protections, and no prompt or output is sent to us. On devices without Apple Intelligence the app uses built-in text instead, with no change to anything else.

7. Purchases

The full analysis is unlocked by a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or receive your name, payment method or Apple Account details. The app reads only whether an entitlement exists on your device in order to unlock the corresponding features, and the "Restore purchases" control asks Apple to re-synchronize that entitlement.

8. Sharing your result

If you tap "Share my result", FaceCheck generates a summary card image and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. What happens next is entirely your choice and is governed by whichever app or service you select. Nothing is shared unless you initiate it.

9. Your rights and controls

Privacy laws including the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete, port and restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to its sale. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete, and nothing to sell. The three preferences described in section 4 are under your sole control and are removed by deleting the app.

If you believe we hold data about you, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will respond within [RESPONSE PERIOD, e.g. 30 days].

10. Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Photos and analysis results are held only in device memory for the duration of your session. Local preferences persist on your device until you delete the app.
