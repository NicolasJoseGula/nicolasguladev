What actually matters in an EPUB reader

Strip away the feature lists and a great mobile e-reader comes down to five things:

  • Clean typography. Comfortable type, good margins, justified text that doesn't fight you.
  • Night-friendly themes. Light, sepia and true dark — because most phone reading happens in bed.
  • Your exact page, always. Open the app, land where you left off. Zero ceremony.
  • Easy imports. Add an EPUB from Files, iCloud or anywhere in two taps.
  • No noise. No store pushing bestsellers, no social layer, no badges begging for attention.

Plenty of apps check those boxes. The problem is what happens next: your beautiful reader sits on the home screen, unopened, three icons away from Instagram. The e-reader market's dirty secret is that the competition isn't other readers — it's the feed.
