FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Block&Read?
An app for iPhone and iPad that combines an app blocker with a reader for EPUB, PDF and comics. You choose the apps that eat your time, and when you try to open one, a book appears instead. Read a few pages to your daily goal and your apps unlock. Learn more
How does read-to-unlock work?
Tap a blocked app and Block&Read intercepts it, opening your current book at your exact page. Hit your daily reading goal, a page or two is enough, and every blocked app unlocks for the day. Learn more
Which apps can I block?
Any app on your iPhone: social media, video, games. Free blocks one app, forever. Premium blocks every app and adds schedules, so the block turns itself on without you. Learn more
What is Strict Mode?
The no-easy-way-out switch. Other blockers let you wait out a timer or tap "ignore". With Strict Mode, the only way to your apps is through the pages. Learn more
Can I read my own books?
Yes: EPUB, PDF and comics in CBZ. Your first book of your own is free, forever. Premium lets you bring as many as you like and search 75,000+ public-domain classics. Learn more
Can I highlight and take notes?
Yes. Select a passage to highlight it in one of eight colors, or any color you pick, and attach a note. Bookmarks save a page and bring you back to it, even after you change the text size.
What books are included for free?
81 public-domain classics in English and Spanish (Austen, Brontë, Dickens, Cervantes and more) ready to read at no cost. Premium adds search across 75,000+ classics. Learn more
Does it work on iPad?
Yes. The shelves, the reader and the widgets are laid out for the larger screen, with up to twelve books in the extra-large widget.
Does Block&Read track my data?
No. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Everything stays on your device or in your own private iCloud.
How much does Block&Read cost?
Free covers one blocked app, one book of your own, the classics shelf, streaks and reminders. Premium is USD 4.99 a month, USD 24.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or USD 39.99 once. Prices vary by country; the App Store shows yours. See pricing
Is there a one-time purchase?
Yes. Lifetime gives you everything in Premium for a single payment: no subscription and nothing to renew.
Can Block&Read reduce my screen time?
That's the point, but instead of just cutting you off, it swaps scrolling minutes for reading pages. The reach-for-the-phone habit stays; it just delivers chapters now. Learn more
Does it work for studying?
Yes. Block your distracting apps during study sessions, and load your course reading as PDFs or EPUBs so the blocker itself becomes the study tool. Highlights and notes stay with the text. Learn more
Is Block&Read available in Spanish?
Yes. The app speaks Spanish, and the classics shelf comes in English and Spanish, with a dedicated Spanish library: Don Quijote, La Celestina, Lazarillo de Tormes and more. Learn more
