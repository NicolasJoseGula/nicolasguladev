How it works

Four steps. One honest day at a time.

A plan is ready before you finish the first minute.

1

Pick what to fix

Mornings, discipline, health, focus, screen time or sleep. Your plan is built around them.

2

Set your alarm

Choose the time. It rings every day with a question that fits what you picked.

3

Choose one habit

Small, daily and yours. Add a photo, pick a color, tick it off.

4

Watch the year fill

Every kept day lights a square. Miss one and it shows. Keep going and the year turns to color.

FAQ

Questions, answered. Fitting.

What is Fix Your Life?
An iPhone app that combines alarms and habits. Each alarm rings with a question you answer in one line, like “Are you doing everything necessary to achieve your goals?”. Habits carry your own photos and colors, and every day you keep one lights a square on its grid. Learn more
How do habits work in Fix Your Life?
Add the things you want to keep, every day or on chosen days. Tick a habit off and its streak grows. Each habit draws itself as a grid of weeks in its own color, and the year view shows twelve months on a single screen.
Can I use my own photos for habits?
Yes. Put a photo of your own on any habit: the dog you walk, the plant you water, the gym you swore you'd go back to. Or pick one of twenty symbols and one of eight colors.
Can I fix a day I forgot to mark?
Yes. Open a habit's calendar and mark or unmark any day already lived. The record should be true, not flattering.
How is Fix Your Life different from a normal alarm clock app?
A normal alarm interrupts your sleep. Fix Your Life interrupts your autopilot: its alarms ring with a question you face and answer, instead of a sound you silence and forget. Learn more
What kind of questions does the alarm ask?
Thirty-four questions in six packs: Essentials, Ambition, Time and Mortality, Identity, Honest Mirror and Aliveness. Pick one, write your own, or let Surprise mode choose a different one for each day of the week. Learn more
Can I write my own alarm messages?
Yes, and it's free. Write and save your own questions and reuse them across alarms. Learn more
Does Fix Your Life have iPhone home screen widgets?
Yes. Widgets show your streak and the question of the day on your home screen. Learn more
How much does Fix Your Life cost?
Free to download, and one alarm and one habit are free forever with every feature. Premium unlocks unlimited alarms and habits: yearly at $17.99 with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers, weekly at $4.99, or a one-time Lifetime purchase at $39.99. Prices are in USD and vary by country. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; manage them anytime in your App Store settings.
What languages is Fix Your Life available in?
Forty-five languages, and you can switch between them from Settings at any time.
Does Fix Your Life show ads or sell my data?
No ads, ever. There is no account and no sign-up. Your alarms, habits, photos and answers stay on your device. Privacy policy
