FAQ

Questions, answered. Fitting.

What is Fix Your Life?
Fix Your Life (FYL) is an iPhone alarm app that replaces the generic beep with confronting, reflective questions — like “Are you doing everything necessary to achieve your goals?”. When the alarm rings you answer in one line, the answer is saved to your journal, and every day you show up grows your streak. Learn more
How is Fix Your Life different from a normal alarm clock app?
A normal alarm interrupts your sleep. Fix Your Life interrupts your autopilot. Its alarms ring until you face a question and answer it — no snooze-and-forget, no math puzzles, just a moment of honest reflection. Learn more
What kind of questions does the alarm ask?
Short, confronting ones: “Are you doing everything necessary to achieve your goals?”, “Are you spending time on things you truly want?”. Pick a preset, write your own, or let Surprise mode pull a different question every day. Learn more
Can I write my own alarm messages?
Yes — write and save unlimited custom messages and reuse them across alarms. Personal questions in your own words hit harder than any preset. Learn more
How does the streak and heatmap work?
Answer at least one alarm question a day and your streak grows. The Journal's heatmap shows every day you showed up, so consistency becomes visible — and worth protecting. Learn more
Does Fix Your Life have iPhone home screen widgets?
Yes. Widgets show your current streak and the question of the day right on your home screen, so the practice confronts you before you even open the app. Learn more
Is Fix Your Life a journaling app?
It's a one-line-a-day journal built on top of alarms. Every answer is saved with its question and date, you can reread everything in the Journal, and share any reflection as a clean black & white image. Learn more
Can Fix Your Life help me stop living on autopilot?
That's exactly what it's built for. Research suggests we spend almost half the day on mental autopilot. A well-timed question forces a moment of awareness — and answering it anchors you in the present. Learn more
How much does Fix Your Life cost?
Free to download. Premium unlocks unlimited alarms, custom messages and every feature — weekly with a 3-day free trial, or yearly (best value). Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; manage anytime in your App Store settings.
What languages is Fix Your Life available in?
Six languages — so it can confront you in the words that hit home.
Does Fix Your Life show ads or sell my data?
No ads. Ever. Your reflections are personal, and the app stays a clean, distraction-free black & white space with nothing competing for your attention.
